About Desi Utility

Desi Utility is a simple collection of free browser-based tools for everyday tasks. Our goal is to make common calculations and text tasks accessible, clear and easy to use on desktop and mobile devices.

We are building the site gradually. Tool availability and features may change as the project grows.
